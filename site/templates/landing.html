How it works ↓

How it works

Your realm emits an event. gnotif finds the matching trigger and sends a push to the browser that opted in.

  1. 01 / Emit

    Something happens on chain

    A message, a new turn, an update. Your realm emits an event.

  2. 02 / Match

    A trigger picks the recipient

    A trigger in the gnotif registry says which event to watch and who to notify.

  3. 03 / Notify

    The browser gets the push

    Your dapp’s service worker shows it, even when the tab is closed.
